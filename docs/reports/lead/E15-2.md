## 2. What was built

The principal's **Data** screen, and the two server verbs it needed.

**Three tabs on a segmented control**, `.hsts-segmented`, built from `DataTab.values()` — the
house idiom the reports screen's dimension picker uses. No `TabPane`: I looked, and there is no
`TabPane` anywhere in `client/`, so the segmented control is the codebase's only tab idiom.

| Tab | Verb behind it | What a row carries |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | `BANK_LIST` — **existing, unchanged, unwidened** | id, stem, topic, difficulty, course, version, date. No answer key: `BankQuestionRow` has never carried one |
| Exams | `DATA_EXAMS_GET` — **new** | display id, name, course, author, version count, last written |
| Results | `DATA_RESULTS_GET` — **new** | sitting, course, closed date, mean, median, sigma, pass rate, participants |

**Per tab: a text filter and a course picker.** The text filter is `contains`,
case-insensitive, `strip()`ped, matched against everything on the row a person would recognise it
by (ids, names, topic, course, author, code). The course picker is **derived from the rows in
hand** rather than fetched, which has two consequences worth stating: there is no course-list verb
for this role to call, so nothing new was invented for it; and the dropdown can never offer a
course that would filter the list to nothing, which is the dead end PRD §4.1 forbids. No date
pickers, no pagination.

**Four designed empty panels**, in the voice of the reports screen's three: one per tab (the bank
is empty / no exams have been written / no sittings have finished), plus a distinct **"nothing
matches that filter"**. The fourth is the one that earns its keep — a principal who has typed
something and is told the bank is empty will believe it.

**T-11.3 asserted rather than eyeballed.** `DataBrowserInteractionTest.nothingOnTheScreenWrites`
boots the app, opens the screen, and asserts that under `.principal-data` there is not one
`.button`, that the only `.toggle-button`s are the three tabs, and that the only `.text-field` is
the filter box. `DataCopy.READ_ONLY_NOTE` is also on screen, because a screen that simply has no
buttons is indistinguishable from one whose buttons have not been built yet.

## 3. E15.1 is ticked too, and here is why ⚑

E15.1 reads "Principal read-only services: browse bank, exams, results". Its note said the report
half was shipped and the other two were not E15's problem. They were this pass's problem, and all
three now exist:

- **bank** — `BankReadHandlers`, untouched. She has been on `BANK_LIST`, `QUESTION_GET`,
  `QUESTION_VERSIONS` and `QUESTION_IMAGE_GET`'s role list since E6 (F9.3, BANK contract §2/§3).
  **No verb was added for this tab and none was widened**, which is the single most important
  thing to check in this diff. `VerbTest` asserts that no `DATA_QUESTIONS_GET` exists.
- **exams** — `DATA_EXAMS_GET`, new.
- **results** — `DATA_RESULTS_GET`, new.

Both new verbs are `Authorization.requireRole(caller, PRINCIPAL)` **and nothing else**, on the
reasoning §2 of the REPORTS contract already froze: her scope is the school, so a scope check
could only ever pass. Both reach the database only through `ReportData`, whose every method is
still a read — so **S-7 stays structural**: a mutating verb for her role cannot be written without
first adding a method to that interface, in a file whose javadoc says why it has none.

Both take **no payload at all** (`null`, not an empty record). There is no field for a client to
set, so there is nothing to widen and neither verb has a `VALIDATION` path.

| Verb | Caller | Request payload | OK payload |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DATA_EXAMS_GET` | principal | *(none)* | `DataExams` |
| `DATA_RESULTS_GET` | principal | *(none)* | `DataResults` |

**Why this contract owns them rather than a fourth one.** It already owns her role. §2's rule is
exactly the rule these two need, and a fourth contract restating it would be a second place for it
to drift. The DTOs live beside the report DTOs in `common/dto/report` for the same reason.

New DTOs, all `Serializable` records, all wire-guard clean (no correctness-flavoured component, so
`WireDtoLeakGuardTest`'s licence map needed **no new entry** — checked by running the guard family
early):

- `DataExamRow(displayId6, examName, courseCode, courseName, authorName, versions, lastVersionAt)`
- `DataExams(List<DataExamRow>)`, with `EMPTY`
- `DataResults(List<ReportRow>)`, with `EMPTY`

**`DataResults` reuses `ReportRow` unchanged**, and that is the design rather than a shortcut. A
sitting the principal browses and a sitting a report compares are the same thing seen twice; a
second row record would be a second place for a divisor, a pass mark or a decile width to be
chosen — precisely what §4 of the contract refused when it reused `ResultStatistics`. The
practical payoff is in `JpaReportStoreContract.browseAndReportsShareOneDefinition`: the browse and
the reports are asserted to return the same sittings, because they share one `REPORTABLE` clause,
so **H15.2's cancelled-sitting exclusion is inherited rather than restated**.

`authorName` and no author **id**: this row is read, and an id would be the beginning of a request
that acted on her.

## 5. Decisions

**Ordering is deliberately opposite to the reports'.** `DataResults` is **newest first**; a report
is oldest first. A browse is a filing cabinet and the row being looked for is usually the most
recent; a comparison is a trend and reads left to right. `InMemoryReportStore.allClosedSittings`
is faithful about this (it reverses), so a query with the wrong `order by` cannot pass its own
test.

**The exam row's name is the LATEST version's; a sitting's is the RELEASED version's.** The two
lists deliberately disagree about a renamed exam, and each is right about its own question:
"which exams exist" is a question about the exam, "what did the students sit" is a question about
the sitting. Same reasoning `AuthoredExam` already carries.

**The bank arrives a page at a time and is shown as one list.** `BANK_LIST` is paginated because a
teacher's browse is (E6.5); this screen is not (PRD §6). So `DataSession` asks for page after page
at `MAX_PAGE_SIZE` and appends. The loop is bounded at `MAX_BANK_PAGES = 20` (2000 questions), and
`DataCopy.TOO_MANY_QUESTIONS` says so if the bound is ever hit — a loop that asks a server for
pages until it says stop is a loop, and an unbounded one is a client that hangs on a server
answering nonsense. Both the joining and the bound are tested; the seed's 40 questions arrive in
one request.

**Filters are client-side on all three tabs, including the bank's course code.** The bank's could
have been a server filter (`BankListRequest.courseCode` exists and is intersected server-side),
and it deliberately is not, so all three tabs narrow the same way. The lists are school-sized, and
a filter that travelled would be a field a client could set — the one thing §4 refuses to put on
this role's wire.

**A tab that failed is asked again when it is next opened; a tab that succeeded never is.** That
is not the manual refresh NFR-18 forbids: with no reload button on the screen, coming back is the
only way out of a dropped connection. Tested both ways.

**Filters are per tab and survive a tab switch.** Coming back to a list to find the text you typed
gone would silently undo a decision she made.

**`ReportEngine.toRows` widened from `private` to package-private**, not copied. Same move, same
reasoning as `FrozenStatistics` being widened for E15: the unusable-column drop and the
participant count are decisions, and a sitting reading one way on the browse and another in a
report is exactly what one mapping with two callers prevents.

**`ReportData` grew rather than a second seam appearing beside it.** S-7's structural claim is
"the principal's whole feature is an interface with no write method on it", and that stays true
only while everything her role can reach arrives through that one file. A `DataBrowseData` beside
it would be a second file to check.

## 6. Deviations from the brief, stated ⚑

1. **The brief said "one dropdown falls out naturally (e.g. course)" — it does, but it is derived
   from the loaded rows rather than fetched.** There is no course-list verb this role can call
   (`COURSES_FOR_USER` does not exist as a verb; the client's course list comes from
   `LoginResult.courses`, which is empty for a principal). Deriving it needed no new verb and
   cannot produce a dead-end option. Flagged because "a dropdown built from the data" is a real
   design choice and not obviously the one intended.

2. **The Questions tab opens no detail pane.** BANK contract §7 ruling 2 says the principal *does*
   see the answer key through `QUESTION_GET`, so a detail pane would be legitimate. It was left
   out on the brief's "decide conservatively (less data)" instruction and because T-11.1 asks that
   she can *browse* the bank school-wide, which the list does. **If the lead wants the pane, it is
   `QUESTION_GET` (already hers) plus a detail panel — no wire change at all.**

3. **`RoleNav.ROUTE_DATA` was left in place** rather than deleted, even though that block's javadoc
   says enabled ids leave the list. `ROUTE_REPORTS` was left the same way by E15.4 and `Routes.DATA`'s
   javadoc references the constant by name, as `Routes.REPORTS`'s does. Following the immediate
   precedent rather than the javadoc; a one-line cleanup if the lead prefers the javadoc.

## 7. The one contract ambiguity, decided conservatively and flagged ⚑

**What may a principal's exam row carry — specifically, the exam's approval status?**

`DataExamRow` **omits it**. The argument for omitting: approval is a workflow between an author
and her coordinator (F4.1), and "rejected twice, and here is the reason" is a fact about two
members of staff rather than a fact about the school's exam catalogue. The argument against:
spec 7.3.1 gives her read-only access to all data *as entered*, and a version's status is entered
data — **which is the same argument that decided BANK §7 ruling 2 in the opposite direction, for
the answer key.**

The two are not obviously alike (an answer key is content; a rejection reason is a judgement about
a colleague), but the ruling should be the lead's. It is written up as §6.2 of the amendment.
**If she should see it, the change is one component appended to `DataExamRow` and one column on
the Exams tab. Nothing else moves.**

Also omitted from the exam row, without the same doubt: questions, answer key, teacher
instructions, question count. The first two are the rule every wire record here obeys; the last
would have cost a second grouped query for a number the Exams tab does not need.

## 8. Repository additions, for Member A

Added under TEAM_SPLIT rule 5 — repositories grow with their callers — each with its consumer
named in its javadoc, and each contract-tested on **both engines** in `JpaReportStoreContract`.
**Two queries, both called; nothing existing was changed.**

`ExamRepository`:

- `findAllSummaries(session)` → `List<SchoolExam>` — every exam in the school, with its course,
  its author's display name and its **latest** version's name, number and date, through the same
  correlated `max(versionNo)` subquery `findAuthoredSummaries` uses (so an exam still in draft or
  rejected is listed rather than dropped, which a join to "the approved version" would do).
  **Deliberately a second query rather than `findAuthoredSummaries` with a nullable author**: a
  scoped read whose scope can be switched off with a `null` is one call site away from serving a
  teacher the whole school, and S-35's guarantee is that that query has an author in its `WHERE`
  clause and cannot be asked not to.

`ExecutionRepository`:

- `findAllReportRows(session)` → `List<ExecutionReport>` — the shared `REPORTABLE` clause with **no
  subject filter appended**, newest first. Reusing that constant is the point: "closed, with
  statistics frozen" keeps one home, so the browse and the reports cannot disagree, and H15.2 is
  inherited.
- `NEWEST_FIRST` — one private query fragment beside the existing `OLDEST_FIRST`, with a comment
  saying why the two exist.

New projection: `SchoolExam` (the school-wide sibling of `AuthoredExam`; it carries the author's
name and the version count, which an author's own list has no use for).

## 11. What the lead double-checks

In order of how expensive they would be to get wrong:

1. **§7's ruling** — whether the principal's exam row should carry approval status. It is the one
   thing in this pass that is a judgement rather than a derivation, and it points the opposite way
   from BANK's ruling 2.
2. **That `BANK_LIST` really was not widened.** The whole Questions tab rests on her already being
   on that verb. `BankReadHandlers` is untouched in this diff; confirm by looking for it in §9 and
   not finding it.
3. **`HSTSServer`'s reports line, now two statements.** It is the only rewritten existing
   statement. The two features share one `JpaReportStore` on purpose (one `REPORTABLE` definition);
   if that ever looks like an accident, `browseAndReportsShareOneDefinition` is the test that says
   it is not.
4. **`ReportEngine.toRows` is package-private now.** Deliberate, and the javadoc says why. If a
   third consumer appears it should call this and not copy it — the `FrozenStatistics` precedent.
5. **The bank's page loop and its bound.** It is the only place this screen loops on the network.
   The bound is 20 pages and the sentence that fires at it is asserted.
6. **The course dropdown is derived, not fetched** (deviation 1). It cannot offer a dead end, but
   it also cannot offer a course that exists and has no rows on that tab — which is the right
   behaviour here and the *opposite* of what E15.5 decided for the reports' subject picker, where
   an empty subject is listed with a count of zero. The difference is defensible (a report's
   picker is choosing a subject; this one is narrowing a list you can already see) but it is an
   asymmetry between two screens the same person uses, and worth knowing before somebody reports
   it as a bug.
7. **E15.1 was ticked by this pass** (§3), which the brief did not ask for. If the lead would
   rather it stay open until she has walked it, one box unticks.
