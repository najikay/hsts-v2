## What was reported

During the acceptance run, `omer.katz`'s checked form was reported as showing **no "Not answered"
questions**.

That would have been a real defect on the one path that exists because of your 9.5 ruling, so I
left the case unrecorded rather than guessing. A rendering fault and a refusing gate need

the form opened and marked four questions wrongly; a "not available" sentence meant a gate
refused and the seed was wrong.

## What was actually the case

Checked against the reseeded database, read-only, no screen involved.

**All three checked-form gates pass** for that grade: the execution is `CLOSED`, the grade is
`APPROVED`, ownership is the query. The attempt carries **3 answer rows on a 7-question paper** —
§9.1.1's four absences, present in the database as absences rather than as zeros.

Then the production assembler was run against that data — `CheckedFormService` → `GradeReviewService`
→ `CheckedFormCopy`:

```
header      : 45 / 100
attempt line: Time ran out — submitted automatically · 75 minutes
q1 11001  chosen=1     ->  Correct        15 / 15
q2 11002  chosen=2     ->  Correct        15 / 15
q3 11005  chosen=1     ->  Correct        15 / 15
```

Four unanswered, three correct, 45. Exactly what the amendment was for — and the attempt line
says what happened rather than naming a database state.

**A false alarm.** Most likely a different student's form still open: `maya.levi` answered
everything, and hers correctly shows none.
